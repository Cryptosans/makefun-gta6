SOLARA BAY

An open-world crime story. Steal it. Drive it. Survive it.

Rio Vela is back in Solara Bay, broke and owed. Six jobs stand between him and the big score.

On foot: WASD to move, Shift to run, E to steal a car or start a mission at a pink marker, F or click to punch, Q to draw the pistol, R to reload.

Driving: W/S to accelerate and brake, A/D to steer, E to get out. Stealing a car and hurting locals raises your wanted level — the Solara Bay PD sends one cruiser per star.

Missions: follow the yellow beacon and the minimap diamond. Progress saves automatically; Continue picks up where you left off.

An original game — city, characters, story, art and sound all made for this entry. No Rockstar material.
