SOLARA BAY

Sun. Sand. Stolen cars. Rico Vega is back on the streets of Solara Bay — a 5×5-block open-world crime game.

Fresh out of the Solara docks, Rico owes Mara the fixer one last job. Six missions stand between him and the crown of the city. Steal cars, dodge the Solara PD, and take down the Vipers — or end up just another name on the pier.

WASD / arrows — drive or walk  ·  E — enter / steal / exit car  ·  Space — handbrake

Mouse — look  ·  Click — shoot pistol  ·  Shift — run  ·  M — start mission  ·  R — retry mission / reload  ·  Esc — pause

Follow the gold beacon and the minimap. Crimes raise your wanted level — break line of sight from the police to cool off. Cash pickups drop from defeated enemies.

An original game. No affiliation with Rockstar Games or the Grand Theft Auto series. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser.
